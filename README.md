One-line apology from an office printer to the people who keep jamming it,
feeding it cheap paper, and swearing at it.

**Entry (113 characters):**

> Sorry I jammed during your big presentation. In my defence, your slides were the first thing to bore me to death.

[The Printer Apology on make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-the-printer-apology-60229153)

## How it works

`main.py` writes the entry text (no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`.
Output resolution order: `ENTRY_OUT` (file or directory) → `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` →
`/output/entry.txt` → `./entry.txt` as a last resort.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
